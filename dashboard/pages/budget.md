title: Budget vs actual

<Alert status="info">
All data on this page is synthetic. The budget is generated by the same script as the ledger. Amounts are in NIU (normalized index units).
</Alert>

The budget covers FY2025 and FY2026 by account, cost centre and month. Actuals are mapped to reporting lines by the rule seed, so a mis-posted row counts against the line it belongs to. The partial month is left out.

A quarterly miss is a line and cost centre that is adverse by more than 10 percent of budget and by more than 3,000 NIU in a quarter, with all three months closed. Adverse means an expense over budget or revenue under it.

```sql misses
select quarter_label as quarter_starting, line_name as line, cost_center_code as cost_centre, budget_amount as budget,
    actual_amount as actual, adverse_amount as adverse, adverse_pct
from larkspur.budget_misses
order by quarter_starting, adverse desc
```

<DataTable data={misses} rows=30>
    <Column id=quarter_starting title="Quarter starting" />
    <Column id=line />
    <Column id=cost_centre title="Cost centre" />
    <Column id=budget fmt="#,##0" />
    <Column id=actual fmt="#,##0" />
    <Column id=adverse fmt="#,##0" />
    <Column id=adverse_pct title="Adverse %" fmt="pct1" />
</DataTable>

```sql misses_by_quarter
select quarter_label as quarter_starting, count(*) as misses
from larkspur.budget_misses
group by quarter_label
order by quarter_label
```

<BarChart
    data={misses_by_quarter}
    x=quarter_starting
    y=misses
    title="Budget misses by quarter"
    subtitle="Synthetic data. Count of line and cost centre pairs."
/>

## Store wages against budget

```sql wages
select month_start, cost_center_code as cost_centre, variance_pct
from larkspur.budget_store_wages
order by month_start
```

<LineChart
    data={wages}
    x=month_start
    y=variance_pct
    series=cost_centre
    yFmt="pct1"
    title="Store wages: actual against budget, by store"
    subtitle="Synthetic data. Percent above (positive) or below budget."
